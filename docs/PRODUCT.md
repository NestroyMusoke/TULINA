# Product constitution

Tulina means “we have” in Luganda. Its promise is: **One clinic is empty. The district isn’t.**

Tulina helps district health teams redistribute existing medicine safely. It discovers imbalances in the background, produces a policy-checked proposal, leaves authority with a named human approver, and carries proof of authorization and delivery across an internet outage.

## Product laws

1. No medicine moves because a model said so. Deterministic policy gates and a human DHO approval are mandatory.
2. A suggestion must explain stock, expiry, coverage, route, cold-chain, and authorization evidence.
3. Offline is a supported operating state. The receiving device verifies locally and queues a signed receipt.
4. Reconciliation changes inventory exactly once. Duplicate, tampered, expired, misaddressed, malformed, and conflicting records are rejected or quarantined.
5. Synthetic operational data is always labeled; no patient data is collected.
6. The primary experience is operational, not conversational.

## Human language

The UI says **Found nearby**, **Tulina Note**, **District View**, **Safe to receive**, **Received offline — checking in when connected**, **Delivery confirmed**, and **Needs human review**. Technical evidence belongs in “How Tulina decided” and the audit view.
